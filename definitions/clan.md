# clan

**Status:** working — may not be a useful rank, see Q2

## Working definition

A group of pods that share part of an acoustic repertoire — a set of discrete calls held
in common — taken to reflect shared maternal ancestry. Clans are defined by what the
animals sound like, not by who they travel with.

## Source

Working definition, editors of this repository, 2026-07, paraphrasing standard usage for
resident killer whales. Needs a citation and expert confirmation.

## Scope notes

- The seed data asserts that the Southern Residents comprise a single acoustic clan
  (J clan) containing J, K and L pods. **This is stated with low confidence and needs
  checking.**
- Applies to resident ecotypes only.

## What it is not

**Not a pod, and not an association group.** Clan membership is a claim about repertoire
rather than about travelling together, which makes it the one rank here defined by
acoustics — and therefore the one most directly relevant to what a hydrophone moderator
can actually hear.
