# membership

**Status:** working (usable and in force, but not yet confirmed by a domain expert) — and the most consequential definition here.

## Working definition

An individual is a **member** of the matriline it was born into, and of every group that
matriline belongs to, for the whole of its life. Membership is genealogical: it is
determined by descent, not by who an animal is observed travelling with.

## Source

Working definition, editors of this repository, 2026-07. Not inherited from any
published source, and it deliberately narrows the everyday usage. Needs expert review.

## Scope notes

- A newborn is a member of its mother's matriline from birth.
- Membership ends only at death, or — for ecotypes where dispersal occurs — when the
  individual leaves the natal social unit permanently.
- Where an individual's matriline is unknown but its pod is known, record membership at
  the pod level. Do not invent an intermediate group.
- Group-to-group membership follows the same rule and *can* change: a matriline may be
  reassigned between pods, or split.

## What it is not

**Not association.** L87 (Onyx) was born to L32 in L pod, and after his mother died he
travelled with K pod and then for years with J pod. Under this definition L87 is an
L pod animal, permanently. His travel with J pod is real and important, and it is
recorded in OrcaSound and SalishSea.io as a property of encounters — not here.

This is the distinction most likely to cause confusion, because in ordinary speech "L87
is with J pod" and "J17 is in J pod" use the same words for different kinds of claim.

**Not co-occurrence.** Two matrilines heard on the same hydrophone at the same time have
no membership relationship to each other.

## Why it was defined this way

Defining membership as observed association would make it change constantly, make it
depend on who happened to be looking, and require the register to ingest sighting data
to stay current — which is the scope boundary in
[ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-no-observations.md). See
[ADR-0005](../decisions/0005-membership-is-genealogical.md) for the full argument.

## Open questions

- Does a matriline survive the death of its matriarch as the same entity, or become a
  new one? This determines whether fission is recorded as a temporal change or as a
  deprecation and re-minting.
- For Bigg's, what evidence establishes that dispersal has occurred, as opposed to a
  long absence? Whoever curates Bigg's data needs a rule they can apply consistently.
- If the community's working use of "pod" turns out to be predominantly associational,
  this definition inverts and ADR-0005 needs to be rewritten. Worth checking early.
